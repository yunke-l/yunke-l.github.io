Mini Mobile Robot is a hands-on systems project where software has to respond to physical constraints. That changes how you think about feedback loops, reliability, and the cost of small implementation mistakes.

## What I focused on

- Translating behavior ideas into concrete motion and control logic.
- Iterating through testing cycles where observation matters as much as code.
- Building a stronger intuition for systems that interact with the physical world.

## Why it matters

Even though this is different from frontend engineering, it strengthened my engineering habits. Robotics work rewards careful reasoning, incremental changes, and respect for real-world constraints, which carry over well into software projects.
