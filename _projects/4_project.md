Soccer Team explores a more structured application scenario where the product has to keep track of entities, relationships, and updates over time. That makes it a useful exercise in application logic rather than just page presentation.

## What I focused on

- Modeling information clearly enough for people to understand the domain quickly.
- Organizing views around practical tasks instead of decorative UI.
- Building with maintainability in mind so the app can grow without becoming confusing.

## Why it matters

I enjoy projects that require both logic and clarity. They push me to think about how interface structure should mirror the underlying data model, which is a valuable habit for product engineering.
