This project focuses on the client-side experience of a movie review platform. The goal was to make browsing, search, and review flows feel approachable while keeping the interface structured and easy to extend.

## What I focused on

- Building a frontend architecture that could support multiple views without losing clarity.
- Connecting UI states to external movie data and review content.
- Keeping the interface readable across desktop and smaller screens.

## Why it matters

I like projects like this because they sit right at the intersection of interface design and engineering discipline. A strong frontend should not only look polished, it should also make state management, data fetching, and future iteration easier.
